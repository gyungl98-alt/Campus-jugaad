Campus Jugaad FAQ & University Rulebook

75% Attendance condonation, medical certificates, handwritten assignment detection, cold emailing seniors, book swap safety, and how to self-host this app.

Indian universities (jaise AKTU, VTU, Mumbai University, Anna Univ, IPU, DU, aur private colleges) mein 75% UGC/AICTE mandate hai, lekin isme Condonation Bracket hota hai:

  • 75% and Above (Safe Zone): Koi tension nahi, admit card directly mil jayega bina kisi application ke.
  • 65% to 74.9% (Condonation Zone): Agar aap valid medical certificate (doctor prescription) ya College Tech Fest / Sports On-Duty (OD) submit karte hain, toh Principal/HOD 10% tak condone karke admit card release kar dete hain.
  • Below 65% (Critical Danger Zone): Isme HOD ke paas bhi official power nahi hoti jab tak Vice-Chancellor ya Dean se special sanction na mile.
